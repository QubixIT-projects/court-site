ABOUT THE COURT

Built by players, stocked for players.

The Court started as a single stringing bench for a badminton club and grew into an outfitter for badminton, tennis, squash, paddle and table tennis players across Sri Lanka. We carry the brands players actually ask for, and we still string every racket ourselves.

Where it started

Our founder began restringing rackets for teammates between matches, working out of a spare room with a single stringing machine. Word travelled faster than the shop did — players started asking not just for stringing, but for the shoes, shuttles and grips they couldn't find anywhere else on the island.

That's still how we choose what to stock: if enough players ask for it, we bring it in and test it ourselves before it goes on the shelf.

Where we are now

Today we carry rackets and gear across badminton, tennis, squash, paddle and table tennis, alongside the shoes, apparel and accessories that go with them. Every racket we sell can be strung in-house, and our staff play the sports they sell — ask anyone on the floor for a recommendation and you'll get an honest one.

"We don't sell you the most expensive racket. We sell you the one that matches your swing." — Shop floor motto, since day one

What guides us

01

Authentic gear only

Every racket, shoe and ball we sell is sourced directly from the brand or an authorised distributor — no grey-market stock.
